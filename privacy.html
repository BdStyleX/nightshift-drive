Nightshift Drive: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-31

What this game stores on your device

Your settings (sound, graphics, controls), your cars, upgrades, credits and best lap times are saved in your browser or app storage on your own device.

Optional sign-in with Google

If you tap "Login with Gmail", we use Firebase Authentication from Google. We receive your Google display name, email address and profile photo URL. We use them only to show your profile in the game and to link your saved progress to your account.

Cloud save

When you are signed in, your username, credits, owned cars, car customisation, race count and best lap times are stored in Google Cloud Firestore under your account, so you can continue on another device. Only you can read or change your own data.

What we do not do

We do not show ads, do not sell your data, and do not use your data for tracking or profiling. The game does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Deleting your data

Email us at the address below and we will delete your cloud-saved data. Clearing the app or browser storage removes the data stored on your device.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.
